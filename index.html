svg-scrub

Sanitize SVG files pasted from the web and prove the result is clean.

Your report will appear here.

Removed: script, foreignObject and handler elements, metadata, comments, processing instructions, DOCTYPE, on* event handlers, unsafe links, external images, and dangerous styles. Safe drawing content, title and desc, and raster data URI images are kept.

Privacy: this tool runs 100% in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
